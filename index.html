About

A practical curiosity for systems that amplify people.

I explore the intersection of artificial intelligence, developer experience, and durable software architecture.

My GitHub activity ranges from hands-on LLM learning to agent tooling and curated technical resources. The common thread is a desire to understand emerging systems from the inside—and turn that understanding into more useful ways of building.

This portfolio is a living index of those explorations. It will evolve as projects move from questions to experiments and from experiments to working tools.
